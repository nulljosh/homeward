# Homeward Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Homeward is a lost pet network. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Your account.** Sign-in and the things you post are stored in our database on Supabase. You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it.
- **Posts.** Lost and found posts, photos and the area you post in are public so neighbours can help.
- **Email.** Alerts are sent through Resend.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Homeward isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
